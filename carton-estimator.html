How the math works

1

Cartons per product

cartons = ⌈boxes / boxes-per-carton⌉
Boxes you enter are rounded up to whole export cartons.

2

Volume

totalVolume = Σ (cartons × volume-per-carton)
Add up every product's carton footprint.

3

Container fit

Compare total volume to ISO container capacity: 20'GP = 28 m³ · 40'HQ = 65 m³. Whichever fills first wins.

4

Shipping mode

< 2 m³ → Parcel · 2–15 m³ → LCL · > 15 m³ → FCL. Pick the mode your freight forwarder will quote against.
