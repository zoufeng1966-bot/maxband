SS201 Reusable Clip

Tool-free reusable closure — slide on, lock, release by hand.

Stainless steel clips provide a reusable fastening alternative to crimped buckles for temporary installations or applications requiring periodic adjustment. The clip slides onto the wrapped strap loop and locks into position without specialised tools, then releases by hand for removal.

Common uses include prototype assemblies, temporary bracing, exhibition rigging, container sealing for returnable packaging, and any installation where the strap must be repositioned or replaced frequently. Clips are not as strong as crimped ear buckles, so they are best chosen where reusability matters more than maximum joint strength.

Key Features

  • Fully reusable — install and remove without tools
  • Five widths matching the Maxband strap range (1/4" to 3/4")
  • 201 stainless steel for general atmospheric corrosion resistance
  • Hand-operated locking mechanism for fast installation
  • Ideal for temporary and adjustable installations
  • Lower per-piece cost over the life of repeated-use applications

When to Choose a Clip vs Ear Buckle

Same strap width, different closures for different jobs.

Scenario Recommended Closure
Permanent hose clamp on fixed installationEar Buckle (crimped, higher strength)
Field repair sleeve over pipe damageEar Buckle (single-use permanent)
Temporary bracing during constructionClip (reusable, fast to install)
Exhibition / event riggingClip (repositionable, no tools)
Prototype assembly with iterationClip (reusable, no waste)
Container seal for returnable packagingClip (open and re-seal many times)
Joint that needs occasional openingScrew Lock Buckle (bolted, no tool-free)
