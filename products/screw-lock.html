SS201 Screw Lock Buckle

Bolted mechanical closure — tighten, loosen, re-tighten any number of times.

The screw lock buckle delivers a fully reusable, mechanically fastened joint using a stainless steel bolt and nut assembly. Unlike crimped closures, a screw lock joint can be tightened, loosened, and re-tightened indefinitely without specialised banding tooling — all that is needed is a standard wrench.

Five sizes accommodate the full Maxband strap width range from 1/4" (6.4 mm) up to 3/4" (19.1 mm). Typical applications include tank lid clamps, removable panel fastenings, marine equipment mounts, and any installation requiring routine access for inspection or adjustment.

Key Features

  • Fully reusable — unlimited open / close cycles
  • Five sizes for strap widths 1/4" to 3/4" (6.4 to 19.1 mm)
  • Stainless steel bolt and nut assembly — no specialised banding tool needed
  • Visible torque indication — tighten by feel or with a torque wrench
  • Removable and reusable across multiple installations
  • Best choice for joints requiring periodic access or adjustment
