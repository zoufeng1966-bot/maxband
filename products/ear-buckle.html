SS201 Ear Buckle

Two-ear single-use buckle for permanent high-strength strap closure.

The two-ear buckle is the most common single-use closure for industrial banding installations. Each buckle features two pre-formed ears that fold over the wrapped strap during crimping, creating a permanent, high-strength joint with strong vibration resistance.

Both ears must be crimped for full rated holding capacity — crimping only one ear reduces joint strength significantly. Compatible with our complete strap width range, ear buckles are the workhorse fastener for hose clamping, sign fixing, and pipeline repair where the joint will not be reopened.

Key Features

  • Two-ear design delivers higher joint strength than single-ear alternatives
  • Five widths: 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 5/8", 3/4" (6.4–19.1 mm)
  • Precision-formed from 201 stainless steel for consistent crimp performance
  • Single-use permanent closure — vibration-resistant once set
  • Crimped with Maxband Banding Tool (Standard or Giant)
  • Cost-effective general-purpose closure for industrial fastening

How It Works

1

Wrap & Thread

Wrap strap around workpiece, thread both ends through the ear buckle.

2

Tension

Use banding tool to pull strap tight around the workpiece.

3

Crimp Both Ears

Fold and crimp both ears flat for full joint strength.

4

Cut & Finish

Tool shears off excess strap. Permanent, vibration-resistant joint.

Typical Applications

H

Hose Clamping

Permanent clamping of industrial hoses on piping, hydraulics, and exhaust.

S

Sign Mounting

Road signs, traffic signals, and outdoor signage fixed to posts.

P

Pipe Repair

Field clamping of repair sleeves over cracked or leaking pipe sections.

M

Marine Rigging

Onboard cable management and hose securing in non-submerged applications.
