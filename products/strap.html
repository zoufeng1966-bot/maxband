SS201 Stainless Steel Strap

201-grade austenitic stainless steel banding strip — the base material for our complete banding system.

Our SS201 stainless steel banding strap is a versatile fastening strip engineered for industrial clamping, bundling, and securing applications. Manufactured from grade 201 austenitic stainless steel, it provides reliable corrosion resistance in general atmospheric environments at a competitive price point compared to higher nickel grades.

The strap is supplied in coil form for continuous-feed installation with either the standard manual banding tool or the Giant Banding Tool for heavier-gauge work. Five standard widths cover the most common industrial fastening requirements — from small-bore hose clamps up to large-diameter pipe repair and structural bracing.

Key Features

  • 201-grade austenitic stainless steel for general atmospheric corrosion resistance
  • Five standard widths: 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 5/8", 3/4" (6.4–19.1 mm)
  • Standard thickness 0.76 mm (0.030") — heavier options on request
  • Continuous coil form for uninterrupted installation
  • Compatible with all Maxband ear buckles, clips, and screw lock buckles
  • Magnetic response (typical of 201 grade) — detectable by standard stud finders

Two Packaging Options

Each strap width and coil length ships in either packaging format. Choose based on your shipment economics or your installation crew's on-site handling preference.

P

Paper Box

Economical export packaging.

  • Lower per-unit cost — best for high-volume shipments
  • Lighter tare weight reduces air/ocean freight
  • Fibre-based, recyclable in most regions
  • Printed with size, length, batch code for warehouse identification
  • Stackable for palletised export
B

Plastic Box with Handle

Reusable dispenser for job-site work.

  • Integrated carrying handle for mobile installation crews
  • Box doubles as a payout dispenser — coil feeds out cleanly without tangling
  • Reusable across multiple jobs — returnable to warehouse
  • Better protection against moisture, dust, and coil deformation in transit
  • Stackable latches for tidy storage on the truck or in the workshop

Mixed orders of both packaging formats are accepted — specify your preference per SKU when requesting a quotation.
Note: Paper/Plastic dual packaging applies to Strap only. Ear Buckle, Clip, and Screw Lock ship in paper box exclusively.
