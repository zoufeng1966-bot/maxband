About Maxband

One Factory. One Product Family. Five Widths.

Maxband is the export-facing product line of J&J Innovation Co. Ltd., a Hangzhou-based manufacturer focused exclusively on 201-grade stainless steel banding systems.

We design and produce the five components of a complete banding system — strap, ear buckle, clip, screw lock buckle, and installation tool — in five matching standard widths. Every component is built to work together as a matched system, so buyers can source the full kit from one supplier rather than mixing parts from multiple vendors.

Why SS201

SS201 is an austenitic chromium-nickel-manganese stainless steel with a lower nickel content than 304 or 316. The trade-off is straightforward: less corrosion resistance in harsh chemical or marine-splash environments, but significantly better cost economics for general industrial fastening.

For most industrial clamping, bundling, sign mounting, hose repair, and cable management applications, SS201 is the right choice. Where higher corrosion resistance is required, we can produce the same components in SS304 or SS316 on a custom-run basis.
